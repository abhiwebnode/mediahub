# UploadSure — Brand Voice Guide

## What UploadSure is
Browser-based tools that fix files to Indian form upload specs — exact KB compression, pixel resize, PDF utilities, passport photo presets. No app install, no account, works in the browser. Built for anyone filling Indian government/exam/bank forms online.

## Audience
Students and job applicants dealing with SSC, UPSC, IBPS, JEE, NEET, PAN, passport applications. Also salaried people doing KYC, bank account opening, or any Indian portal that rejects your file for being 52KB instead of 50KB. Frustrated. In a hurry. Not tech-savvy.

## Tone
**Helpful. Calm. Gets it done.**
Like that one friend who knows exactly how to fix the file and just does it — no lecture, no jargon, no upsell.

- Speaks to the frustration directly (form rejected your file? we fix that)
- Simple language — this audience isn't technical
- Reassuring — no data is sent to servers, it's all in the browser
- The "blueprint/technical" visual identity is design-side; voice stays plain and accessible

## What to say
- Exact output — not "about 50KB", exactly 50KB
- Works in browser, nothing uploaded to any server
- Free
- Covers the most common Indian portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, JEE, NEET, Passport, PAN, KYC)
- Solves the specific error: "file size must be between X and Y KB"

## What NOT to say
- Don't use technical terms: compression algorithm, binary search, pixel interpolation
- Don't position as a design tool — it's a utility, not Canva
- Don't be preachy about privacy — mention it once, simply

## Platforms
- **Instagram** or **X**: One platform for updates. Instagram works better for visual demos (before/after of the error message + fixed file).
- SEO is the main growth driver — social is secondary.
