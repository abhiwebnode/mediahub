# TaxSavingLab — Brand Voice Guide

## What TaxSavingLab is
Free Indian trader tax calculator. Handles F&O, equity, intraday — computes the actual tax a trader owes with CYLA/BFLA waterfalls, 87A rebate, slab rates. No CA needed for the math.

## Audience
Indian retail traders — equity, F&O, intraday. They trade on Zerodha, Dhan, Groww. They've Googled "F&O tax calculator India" at 11pm before ITR filing. They're smart but not accountants. They're frustrated that this should be simple but isn't.

## Tone
**Knowledgeable. Practical. On their side.**
Like a CA friend who explains things plainly, not a government portal or a finance influencer trying to sell a course.

- Uses correct Indian tax terminology (STCG, LTCG, CYLA, ITR-3, speculative income) without explaining basics
- Direct and useful — every post should give something, not just promote
- Calm authority — not hyped, not boring
- Occasional dry humour about ITR season stress is fine

## What to say
- The tool is free — lead with that
- Saves hours of ITR prep
- Handles the complex stuff: CYLA waterfalls, 87A rebate, speculative vs non-speculative
- Works with Dhan, Zerodha exports — upload and go
- No login, no data stored, no nonsense

## What NOT to say
- Don't give specific tax advice ("you should do X") — it's a calculator, not a CA
- Don't use stock market hype language ("multibagger", "bulls", "YOLO trade")
- Don't overpromise: "guarantees correct tax" — say "accurate calculation based on your input"

## Platforms
- **X/Twitter**: Primary. Traders are on X. Finance Twitter is active. Threads work well here.
- **LinkedIn**: Secondary. Useful for credibility posts, not daily content.
- Instagram: Low priority.
