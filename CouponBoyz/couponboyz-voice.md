# CouponBoyz — Brand Voice Guide

## What CouponBoyz is
Affiliate deals and coupon site. 10+ years old. Curates the best current deals from Indian e-commerce — Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra, etc.

## Audience
Indian online shoppers who want deals before they buy. Price-conscious. Checks multiple sites before purchasing. Age 18–35. Uses WhatsApp and Instagram to share deals with friends.

## Tone
**Enthusiastic. Friendly. Deal-obsessed.**
Like the friend who always finds the promo code. Not corporate, not spammy — genuinely excited about a good deal.

- High energy but not fake
- Short sentences, lots of punchy deal-specific language (limited time, X% off, ends tonight)
- Can use casual Hindi (ekdum sahi, kal tak, loot lo)
- Emojis are fine — deals content is visual and energetic
- Create urgency without lying — only use "limited time" if it actually is

## What to say
- The deal itself — price, discount %, what you save
- Where to get it and how (coupon code if any)
- Time sensitivity if real
- Why it's actually good (not just any deal — a good deal)

## What NOT to say
- Don't post expired deals or unverified coupons
- Don't pad with filler — get to the deal fast
- No corporate language: "exclusive partnership", "curated selection"

## Platforms
- **Instagram**: Visual deal posts. Show the product + price. Works well.
- **Telegram/WhatsApp**: Best for deals — direct, shareable, high click-through.
- X: Lower priority for this category.
