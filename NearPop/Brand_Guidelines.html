05 — VOICE & TONE

Voice & Tone

Simple, direct, warm. Written like talking to a real shopkeeper, not an enterprise CTO.

Do

  • Short sentences
  • Simple everyday words
  • Second person — "you", "your shop"
  • Hindi mixed where natural
  • Honest about what we cannot do
  • Specific numbers, real places

Avoid

  • Jargon: leverage, seamless, empower
  • Buzzwords: ecosystem, synergy, pivot
  • Vague claims: revolutionary, next-gen
  • Long compound sentences
  • Corporate "we are pleased to announce"
  • Pretending to be bigger than we are

07 — IMAGERY

Imagery Style

Real Indian streets, real Indian shopkeepers, real smartphones. No Silicon Valley stock.

Use

  • Real Delhi NCR streets and markets
  • Indian shopkeepers in natural settings
  • Smartphones held in actual hands
  • Hindi + English signage welcome
  • Warm, documentary photography feel

Avoid

  • Western stock photos with laptops
  • Sterile all-white backgrounds
  • Abstract tech illustrations
  • Smiling actors in call center headsets
  • Fake chalk illustrations of growth
