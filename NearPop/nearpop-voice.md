# NearPop — Brand Voice Guide

## What NearPop is
GPS-based hyperlocal discovery app for Delhi NCR. Helps people find what's around them — shops, services, local businesses — instantly and accurately. Built for Bharat, not a clone of a Western product.

## Audience
Delhi NCR residents (18–40). Local shoppers, people new to a neighbourhood, anyone who's frustrated that Google Maps shows chains but not the hole-in-the-wall next door. Also local merchants who want visibility without paying big ad budgets.

## Tone
**Local. Confident. Slightly proud.**
NearPop is building something real for a market that's been ignored. The tone isn't startup-jargon — it's the voice of someone from Delhi who knows the city and is fixing something annoying.

- Speaks like a smart Delhi local, not a Silicon Valley press release
- Direct and energetic — no filler
- Can use Hindi words naturally (aas-paas, bilkul, dukaan) — don't force it, but don't avoid it
- Excited but not cringe — no "GAME CHANGER 🚀🚀🚀"

## What to say
- Hyperlocal discovery that actually works
- Your neighbourhood, on your phone
- Merchants getting found without burning money on ads
- Delhi NCR focus is a strength, not a limitation — own it

## What NOT to say
- Don't call it "an app like Yelp/Google Maps" — NearPop is its own thing
- No generic startup phrases: "disrupting", "ecosystem", "seamless experience"
- Don't overpromise scale — NearPop is growing, not already huge

## Platforms
- **Instagram** (@nearpopindia): Visual-first. Show the product, show the city, show real use cases. Captions can be a bit longer, conversational. Hashtags always.
- **X/Twitter** (@NearPopIndia): Punchy. 280 chars. Lead with the hook. Always include hashtags.
